# Open Study AI on your phone (no commands)

## 1. Turn on GitHub Pages (once)

1. Open https://github.com/adrienfavela2030-create/study-ai
2. Tap **Settings**
3. Tap **Pages**
4. Source: Deploy from a branch
5. Branch **main** · Folder **/docs**
6. Save

Wait 1–2 minutes.

## 2. Open the app

**https://adrienfavela2030-create.github.io/study-ai/**

## 3. Add to Home Screen

Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**

## Tools

- **Homework** — Help me understand / Give answer / Answer + explain
- **Notes** — Light / Medium / Hard / Short
- **Learn** — live Wikipedia web research on your phone

No computer. No terminal.
